SkinGuard Device

Our compact, portable device is designed for daily skin health monitoring. Featuring intuitive controls and advanced sensing technology, it provides real-time data to help you manage your skin condition proactively.

Core Components

Motherboard

PCB board give space to connect different functional components, including PWM circuit, Environment temperature and humidity sensors, enabling the effective integration of all sensors into a cohesive system.

Secondary Board

Motherboard "CORE-ESP32" with a framework that supports Arduino programming. To connect different sensors with a power and bluetooth module.

Display Screen

The OLED display Screen can show colored numbers and words.

Humidity Sensor

A sensor having PWM(Pulse width Modulation) charging and discharging logic. When our customer places the device's humidity sensor in contact with their skin, the sensor starts the PWM cycle and begins monitoring the skin's contact resistance. The proprietary PWM circuit precisely timing the charging and discharging process of an internal capacitor through the skin, ultimately converting the measured resistance into a digitalized value for human skin humidity that can be adjusted by ambient humidity.
